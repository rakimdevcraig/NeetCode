## What I Learned

### Reasoning models are a little slower than non-reasoning models but they're good for multi-step tasks that require deep logic and planning

I was using a reasoning model locally and was wondering why it took so long to even answer a basic prompt like "tell me a joke" but I realized you're not supposed to use them for simple prompts like that

### When an LLM makes a mistake think about what context was missing instead of the model's incompetence

This is the attidude I'm gonna have going forward because LLM's are smart but they aren't perfect and I need to make sure i'm supplying it with everything it needs to get whatever job i'm asking it to get done

### Capitalization adds weight to words

Didn't know this and sometimes I would capitalize random words in my prompt if my caps lock button got hit but I'm gonna be more specific when I do that.
