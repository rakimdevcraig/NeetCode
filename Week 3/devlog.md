## What I Learned

### Transformers

I learned what a transformer is and why it’s often better than RNNs and CNNs for machine translation. Transformers speed up training because they process data in parallel and can connect all tokens in a sequence using attention.

### FastAI (Under the Hood)

In FastAI, I learned what’s happening under the hood when training a model.  
I worked through the training loop and learned:

- **Gradient descent** — the method used to adjust weights
- **Loss functions** — how we measure how wrong a model is
- The math behind calculating loss
- **Learning rate** — why it can’t be too small or too large and how it depends on the model and problem (no one-size-fits-all)
