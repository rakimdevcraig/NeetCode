## What I Learned

### Different models require different prompting approaches

Reasoning models perform better with high-level goals and can be trusted to figure out more complex tasks. Non-reasoning models need very explicit context and specific instructions to help them perform better

### Prompt Caching

You can reduce cost and latency by keeping information that doesn't change between requests at the very beginning of my prompt

### Model Snapshots

If you have an application that uses a specific model you can take a snapshot of it just incase it changes down the line it won't affect your app.

### Model Output

It is not safe to assume the text you want is always located at output[0].content[0].text. This is on par with models being non-deterministic
