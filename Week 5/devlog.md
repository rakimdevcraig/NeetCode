### Stacks

I had heard of the call stack but hadn't really worked with stacks before so doing 5 different problems helped me gain an understanding of them and their purpose. I've also learned that usually when problems involve matching elements like brackets or parentheses its probably a stack problem or when you need to look at something recent like the lowest or highest element thusfar it's probably a stack problem.

### Logging

Just from the things i'm gonna be logging i've learned that in the moment logging doesn't seem like a big deal but when a problem happens such as outage or an attack on your network you need to have logs to go back and see the when and the why as to why whatever happened. Also having logs just to evaluate how your app is performing is key incase incase you need to make performance improvements to your project.

### System Design

After doing our interview I heard some people got asked system design questions so I started learning that this weekend. I interviewed with cloudflare so I learned a little about how they cache a copy of your site. If a user visits your site they check if a copy of your site is on a server nearby because loading that copy will be much quicker than going all the way to the actual server for your website and they store it in many locations so there will usually be a cached version close to your user.
